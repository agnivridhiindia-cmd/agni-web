## Executive Summary

Pal & Sons Agro Pvt Ltd is a specialized agro-processing and packaging venture focused on high-quality cold-press edible oils and value-added agricultural commodities.

## The Challenge

First-generation food processing ventures require rapid statutory setup and rigorous financial planning before approaching banks and private investors. Protracted government registration cycles and incomplete documentation frequently delay plant commissioning and capital disbursements.

## The Agnivridhi Solution

Agnivridhi delivered an end-to-end fast-track incorporation and investor-readiness package:

1. **Fast-Track Incorporation**: Executed corporate registration, PAN, TAN, and corporate filings within a 15-day sprint.
2. **Statutory Registrations**: Secured GST registration and MSME/Udyam certification to qualify for sovereign industrial benefits.
3. **Investor-Ready Modeling**: Built bank-grade Detailed Project Reports (DPR) with cash-flow sensitivity analyses that unlocked institutional seed capital.

## Business Impact & Results

- **15 Days** total execution time from incorporation to full statutory compliance.
- **Investor-Ready Status**: Unlocked initial seed capital and term financing for cold-press processing equipment.
- **18 Direct Jobs** created for local technical operators and agro-packaging staff.
