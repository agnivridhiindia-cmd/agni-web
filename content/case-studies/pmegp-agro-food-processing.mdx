## Executive Context

An agricultural value-add venture in Western Uttar Pradesh required modernized cold-press filtration equipment to package retail-ready unrefined edible oils.

## Structural Barrier

First-generation entrepreneurs often struggle with the multi-tier government subsidy clearance matrix, resulting in portal application rejection or lost margin money subsidies during branch sanction stages.

## Advisory Solution Delivered

1. **PMEGP Project Report Formulation**: Prepared an aligned DPR adhering to the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) and Ministry of MSME benchmarks.
2. **DLTFC Committee Screening Assistance**: Provided query management and project defense before the district task force committee.
3. **Branch Loan Coordination & Subsidy Lock-In**: Synchronized the joint term loan appraisal with the financing public sector bank, securing sanction and immediate margin money escrow release.
