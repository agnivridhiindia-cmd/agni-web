## Executive Context

A mid-market structural steel and heavy engineering manufacturer based in Greater Noida had built strong engineering capabilities but lacked formal institutional accreditation for statutory tenders.

## Structural Barrier

Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) and railway infrastructure procurement guidelines strictly disqualify non-accredited vendors. The client had previously faced tender rejection due to uncertified Quality Management Systems (QMS).

## Advisory Solution Delivered

1. **Shop-Floor Diagnostic Audit**: Assessed calibration schedules, welding inspection checkpoints, and batch chemical test logs across 12 fabrication bays.
2. **QMS Protocol Institutionalization**: Drafted compliant Quality Manuals, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and defect logging mechanisms compliant with ISO 9001:2015 standards.
3. **Audit Defense & Certification**: Guided the operations team through Stage 1 and Stage 2 external registrar audits, securing accredited certification with zero major non-conformances.
