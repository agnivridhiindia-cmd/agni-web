## Executive Summary

Goldi Kirana Store is an essential retail merchant serving local residential and commercial communities, seeking to scale inventory capacity and improve operational profitability.

## The Challenge

Independent retail merchants frequently struggle to negotiate favorable terms from FMCG distributors without bulk upfront purchasing power. Traditional commercial lenders required immovable property collateral, preventing the business from securing working capital through conventional loan products.

## The Agnivridhi Solution

Agnivridhi structured a working capital financing strategy under the MSME CGTMSE framework:

1. **Eligibility & Scheme Matching**: Positioned the application under the CGTMSE retail and wholesale trading coverage window.
2. **Working Capital Assessment**: Prepared 3-year turnover and inventory velocity statements demonstrating repayment feasibility.
3. **End-to-End Bank Liaison**: Handled queries and lender committee documentation through to sanction and credit facility disbursement.

## Business Impact & Results

- **₹18 Lakhs** sanctioned in collateral-free working capital under the sovereign guarantee trust.
- **+28% Monthly Sales**: Higher product variety and consistent stock availability drove immediate revenue gains.
- **Margin Expansion**: Bulk distributor purchasing unlocked direct volume discounts, improving net margins across key product lines.
