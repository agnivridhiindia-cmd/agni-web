## Executive Summary

Vedanta Cosmetics aimed to capitalize on India's burgeoning consumer demand for organic, clean-label personal care products by establishing a dedicated manufacturing and packaging unit.

## The Challenge

Launching an organic cosmetics manufacturing facility requires clean-room formulation equipment, GMP-compliant plant layout, and substantial capital outlay. As first-time entrepreneurs, high debt-servicing during early gestation would have strained working capital severely without sovereign grant subsidies.

## The Agnivridhi Solution

Agnivridhi structured a comprehensive advisory roadmap under the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP):

1. **DPR & Subsidy Calibration**: Formulated the Detailed Project Report adhering strictly to KVIC / PMEGP manufacturing cost ceilings, ensuring maximum subsidy entitlement under the rural/special category window.
2. **DLTFC Committee Preparation**: Prepped the founders for the district-level screening interview with quantitative justifications on employment generation and project viability.
3. **Pre-Disbursement Subsidy Locking**: Coordinated directly with the lending bank branch to ensure margin money was locked in Term Deposit Receipt (TDR) format on the sovereign portal before capital machinery payments were released.

## Business Impact & Results

- **35% Government Subsidy** successfully credited on ₹15 Lakh project cost.
- **Manufacturing Unit Established**: Complete production facility with modern equipment and quality control systems in 45 days.
- **25+ Direct Jobs Created**: Employment opportunities in production, quality inspection, and packaging.
- **Product Line Launched**: 8 SKUs including skincare, haircare, and wellness products distributed in 50+ retail stores across 3 states.
