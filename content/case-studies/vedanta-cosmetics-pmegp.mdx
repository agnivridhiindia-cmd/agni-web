## Executive Context

Vedanta Cosmetics aimed to capitalize on India's burgeoning consumer demand for Ayurvedic and clean-label cosmetics by establishing a dedicated formulation and packaging unit.

## The Financial Challenge

Startup manufacturing enterprises face severe early cash-flow strain. Without government capital subsidy incentives, high bank debt servicing during the gestation and trial-run phase frequently pushes viable ventures into distress.

## The Agnivridhi Turnkey Solution

1. **DPR & Subsidy Calibration**: Formulated the Detailed Project Report adhering strictly to KVIC / PMEGP manufacturing cost ceilings, ensuring maximum subsidy entitlement under the rural/special category window.
2. **DLTFC Committee Preparation**: Prepped the promoter for the district-level screening interview with quantitative justifications on employment generation and project viability.
3. **Pre-Disbursement Subsidy Locking**: Coordinated directly with the lending bank branch to ensure margin money was locked in Term Deposit Receipt (TDR) format on the sovereign portal before capital machinery payments were released.
