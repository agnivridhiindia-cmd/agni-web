## Executive Context

A specialized precision manufacturing enterprise located in the Noida industrial cluster required urgent balance-sheet expansion to fulfill multi-year supply contracts for tier-1 automotive original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). 

## Structural Barrier

Traditional scheduled commercial banks mandated primary collateral coverage equivalent to 100% - “125% of the proposed ₹2.40 Crore capital expenditure loan. With existing real estate properties already encumbered, the promoter faced an impasse that threatened tender eligibility.

## Advisory Solution Delivered

1. **Institutional DPR Engineering**: Prepared a comprehensive financial feasibility model incorporating machine cycle-time efficiencies, operating EBITDA projections, and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) stress-testing across fluctuating raw material costs.
2. **Sovereign Risk Syndication**: Positioned the credit proposal under the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) window, qualifying the borrower for collateral waiver backed by an 85% central guarantee.
3. **Lender Credit Committee Defense**: Directly represented the enterprise during credit evaluation queries, ensuring swift sanction and milestone disbursement.
