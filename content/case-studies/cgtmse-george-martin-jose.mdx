## Executive Summary

George Martin Jose required timely capital to expand commercial operations, enhance purchasing power with suppliers, and stabilize working capital cycles without pledging personal or business assets as security.

## The Challenge

Traditional bank financing mandated collateral and extensive documentation, creating long delays and uncertainty. Accessing growth capital without encumbering residential or commercial property is one of the hardest hurdles for growing enterprises.

## The Agnivridhi Solution

Agnivridhi stepped in to evaluate eligibility under the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) scheme and executed the application end-to-end:

1. **Credit Feasibility & DPR Modeling**: Prepared bank-ready financial projections and debt service capability analysis.
2. **Scheduled Bank Liaison**: Coordinated directly with the lending bank branch to navigate credit appraisals and sovereign guarantee underwriting.
3. **Turnkey Compliance**: Synchronized MSME and statutory documentation so approval was achieved in just 30 days.

## Business Impact & Results

- **₹35 Lakhs** sanctioned in 100% collateral-free working capital debt.
- **30 Days** total turnaround time from file submission to credit sanction.
- **+18%** immediate increase in working capital efficiency, unlocking accelerated business growth.
